QUICK START GUIDE
=================

Installing Your Adobe Video Speed Controller Extension
------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1: Prepare the Extension Files
- All files are ready in the 'adobe-video-speed-controller' folder
- You should have: manifest.json, popup.html, popup.js, content.js, and 3 icon files

STEP 2: Load the Extension in Chrome
1. Open Google Chrome
2. Type chrome://extensions/ in the address bar and press Enter
3. Look for the "Developer mode" toggle in the TOP-RIGHT corner
4. Click it to turn ON Developer mode
5. Three new buttons will appear - click "Load unpacked"
6. Navigate to and select the 'adobe-video-speed-controller' folder
7. Click "Select Folder"

STEP 3: Verify Installation
- You should see a new extension card titled "Adobe Video Speed Controller"
- A green "VS" icon should appear in your Chrome toolbar (top-right, near the address bar)
- If you don't see it, click the puzzle piece icon and pin the extension

STEP 4: Using the Extension

FIRST TIME SETUP:
1. Navigate to your CSP training video page
2. Click the green "VS" icon in your toolbar
3. Set your preferred speed (recommended: 1.5x)
4. Make sure "Auto-apply on page load" is toggled ON (green)
5. Click "Apply"

FROM NOW ON:
- The extension will automatically apply your chosen speed to all videos
- Just load your training page and start watching!
- Click the icon anytime to change speed

QUICK TIPS:
------------

✓ Preset Buttons: Click 1x, 1.25x, 1.5x, 1.75x, 2x, or 2.5x for instant speed changes
✓ Custom Speed: Type any value between 0.25 and 4.0 and press Enter or click Apply
✓ Auto-Apply: Keep this ON to automatically set speed on every video
✓ Speed Memory: Your last used speed is remembered between sessions

TROUBLESHOOTING:
----------------

Problem: Speed not applying?
Solution: Make sure the video has loaded and started playing, then try Apply again

Problem: Extension icon not showing?
Solution: Go to chrome://extensions/, find the extension, and make sure it's enabled

Problem: Not working on certain videos?
Solution: Some players may block speed changes. Try the auto-apply feature or manual application

Problem: Need to reinstall?
Solution: Go to chrome://extensions/, click Remove, then follow Step 2 again

NOTES:
------
- This extension is specifically designed for Adobe Captivate players
- It also works with most other HTML5 video players
- Your privacy is protected - no data is collected or transmitted
- Speed settings are stored locally in your browser only

Enjoy your faster learning!
